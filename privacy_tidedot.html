Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

TideDot is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not require an account and does not collect, store or share your personal information.

Location

TideDot uses your device location to show tide information relevant to where you are and to find the nearest available tidal location.

Location access is only used when you give TideDot permission to access it. You can change or disable location access at any time in your device Settings.

Location Data

Your location may be used to request relevant tide information. TideDot does not store your location or use it to track you.

Data Collection

TideDot does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contact information or other personal data.
